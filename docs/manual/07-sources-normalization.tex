\section{Source Credibility and Normalization}

\subsection{Architecture Independence}

The Beacon scoring system is independent of individual user activity. Scores
are derived from public records, regulatory filings, court decisions, and
agency-generated data. Individual submissions are supplementary signals and
never the sole basis for institutional validity. Unless corroborated by a
public record, they contribute no more than 5\% of a dimensional score.

\subsection{Data Source Credibility Hierarchy}

Conflicting observations are resolved through the following tiers:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{cp{2cm}p{9.2cm}}
\toprule
Tier & Weight & Source description \\
\midrule
1 & 1.00 & Court orders, consent decrees, and signed regulatory enforcement actions. \\
2 & 0.90 & Inspector-general and GAO reports, examination summaries, and sworn congressional testimony. \\
3 & 0.75 & Aggregated CFPB complaints, FTC Consumer Sentinel, and formal state complaints. \\
4 & 0.50 & Self-reported SEC or public filings; cross-validation required. \\
5 & 0.20 & Academic research, third-party studies, and news reporting; corroboration required. \\
6 & 0.05 & Individual platform submissions; supplementary only, never primary evidence. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

MongoDB may serve as a landing zone for heterogeneous documents before
normalization into canonical relational evidence. Graph relationships may be
materialized separately; no storage technology is required by the universal
contract.

\subsection{Normalization}

For a positive metric:
\[
D_{norm}=\mathrm{clamp}_{[0,1]}\left(\frac{D_{raw}-D_{min}}{D_{max}-D_{min}}\right).
\]
For an inverse metric, the bounded result is subtracted from one. Bounds are
versioned by profile and calibrated to a peer class. Extrapolation is not
permitted.
