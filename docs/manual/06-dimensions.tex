\section{Six Dimensions and Metric Registry}

Each dimension is defined once. The operational sub-metric tables, weights,
source lists, and agent specifications come from the Beacon Scoring Engine
specification (source: \texttt{Bias Beacon Documentation.tex}, the second of
the two LaTeX manuals on the maintainer's desktop), reproduced verbatim where
they define behavior. Only the rules that change scoring behavior are
restated here; full metric tables live in the source specification and its
metric registry.

\subsection{Fidelity (\(F\))} % LMCR, PAS, RVR, SOF — weights 0.30/0.25/0.25/0.20; 12-quanta minimum; consent orders are negative evidence; self-reported compliance 0.50 credibility discount; ultra vires finding floors LMCR at 0.20.
Measures whether an institution adheres to its legal mandate, enabling
statutes, and constitutional obligations. Sub-metrics: Legal Mandate
Compliance Rate (LMCR), Policy Adherence Score (PAS), Regulatory Violation
Reversal Rate (RVR), and Statutory Override Frequency (SOF, inverted).
Consent orders count as negative compliance evidence regardless of whether
the institution agreed to them. Self-reported compliance documentation
receives a 0.50 credibility discount unless corroborated. A single court
finding of \emph{ultra vires} action floors LMCR at 0.20 for the affected
period.

\subsection{Consistency (\(C\))} % OVI, PAR, CDS, DRR — cluster-level variance; 15% protected-class disparity triggers equity cross-flag; consent decrees are permanent low-consistency markers until 5-year clean record.
Measures variance in institutional outcomes across substantively similar
cases. Sub-metrics: Outcome Variance Index (OVI, inverted), Precedent
Adherence Rate (PAR), Cross-Case Disparity Score (CDS, inverted), and
Decision Reversal Rate (DRR, inverted). A demographic disparity above 15\%
in any protected class triggers an equity cross-flag regardless of the
overall \(C\) score. Institutions under consent decrees carry permanent
low-consistency markers until a 5-year clean record is established.

\subsection{Transparency (\(T\))} % RDR, RTI, FCR, PAX — clock starts at receipt; in-person-only filing deducts PAX 0.10; contempt within 5 years caps T at 0.40.
Measures whether the institution makes its records, decisions, and
procedures accessible. Sub-metrics: Record Disclosure Rate (RDR), Response
Time Index (RTI), FOIA/Public Records Compliance Rate (FCR), and Proactive
Access Index (PAX). Response time is measured from institutional
\emph{receipt}, not acknowledgment. Institutions requiring in-person filing
only receive an automatic PAX deduction of 0.10. An institution held in
contempt for disclosure failure within the last 5 years cannot score above
\(T = 0.40\).

\subsection{Efficiency (\(E\))} % CRR, PTS, RUI, BCR — administrative closures don't count; peer-class benchmark always displayed; budget shock grace 0.10.
Measures how effectively an institution converts public resources into
mandated outcomes. Sub-metrics: Case Resolution Rate (CRR), Processing Time
Score (PTS), Resource Utilization Index (RUI, net of litigation costs), and
Backlog Clearance Rate (BCR). Administrative closures without documented
outcome do not count. Efficiency scores are always displayed with the
peer-class benchmark visible. Budget cuts exceeding 20\% in a single year
trigger a documented 0.10 grace adjustment for that year only.

\subsection{Equity (\(Q\))} % DDI, GAS, PCOR, ASR — hard floors in 05; n>=100 per cell; never impute demographics; Tier V triggers regulatory referral.
Measures whether the institution delivers its mandate fairly across
demographic groups, geographic areas, and economic strata. Sub-metrics:
Demographic Disparity Index (DDI, inverted), Geographic Access Score (GAS),
Protected Class Outcome Rate (PCOR), and Appeals Success Rate for protected
classes (ASR). Hard floors are defined in Section~5. A minimum \(n = 100\)
per demographic cell is required before statistical claims; the system never
imputes demographic data. A Tier V equity score triggers a mandatory
regulatory referral workflow regardless of the overall Alignment Score.

\subsection{Accountability (\(Ac\))} % SCR, AC, CRR_c, RAR — 1.5x multiplier for proactive correction; recurring identical violations cap Ac at 0.35; RESOLUTION_FRAUD audit trigger.
Measures the institution's capacity to identify its own failures, correct
them, and institutionalize reforms. Sub-metrics: Self-Correction Rate (SCR,
with a \(1.5\times\) multiplier for proactive correction), Audit Compliance
(AC), Complaint Resolution Rate (\(\mathrm{CRR_c}\), distinct from
Efficiency CRR), and Remediation Action Rate (RAR). A pattern of recurring
identical violations (3+ consecutive periods) caps \(Ac\) at 0.35. Complaints
marked resolved without documented resolution trigger a
\texttt{RESOLUTION\_FRAUD} flag and an audit of all complaint-resolution
scores for the preceding 24 months.
