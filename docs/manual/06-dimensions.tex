\section{Six Dimensions and Complete Metric Specifications}

The six dimensions below are the universal institutional fields. The
sub-metrics, formulas, weights, source requirements, agent responsibilities,
and invariants are the detailed Beacon Scoring Engine baseline. They are
preserved here as the operational specification; the newer Alignment
terminology is used for the final result.

\subsection{Dimension 1: Fidelity (\(\Phi_F\))}

\begin{invariantbox}
\textbf{FIDELITY} --- Measures whether an institution adheres to its legal
mandate, enabling statutes, and constitutional obligations. The field
collapses when an institution acts outside statutory authority, systematically
ignores obligations, or applies law selectively.
\end{invariantbox}

\[
F = 0.30\,\mathrm{LMCR}+0.25\,\mathrm{PAS}+0.25\,\mathrm{RVR}+0.20(1-\mathrm{SOF})
\]

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{1.4cm}p{4cm}p{6.7cm}c}
\toprule
Code & Name & Definition and source basis & Wt. \\
\midrule
LMCR & Legal Mandate Compliance Rate & Actions complying with enabling statute divided by total actions; regulatory findings, court orders, IG reports. & .30 \\
PAS & Policy Adherence Score & Internal policy decisions aligned with promulgated regulations; FTC actions, consent orders, correspondence. & .25 \\
RVR & Regulatory Violation Reversal Rate & Violations corrected before external enforcement; IG, voluntary corrections, audit records. & .25 \\
SOF & Statutory Override Frequency & Actions requiring courts or regulators to enforce statutory limits; inverted. & .20 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The Fidelity Signal Parser emits cited quanta with source identifier,
document date, and jurisdiction. Ambiguous compliance characterizations are
flagged for human review. The Field Synthesizer requires at least 12 quanta
from 3 source types, retains outliers, and produces a reproducible score with
confidence interval. Consent orders are negative evidence; self-reported
compliance receives a 0.50 credibility discount unless corroborated. An
\emph{ultra vires} finding floors LMCR at 0.20 for the affected period.

\subsection{Dimension 2: Consistency (\(\Phi_C\))}

\[
C = 0.30(1-\mathrm{OVI})+0.25\,\mathrm{PAR}+0.25(1-\mathrm{CDS})+0.20(1-\mathrm{DRR})
\]

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{1.4cm}p{4cm}p{6.7cm}c}
\toprule
Code & Name & Definition and source basis & Wt. \\
\midrule
OVI & Outcome Variance Index & Variance across substantively similar cases; case databases, FOIA records, dockets; inverted. & .30 \\
PAR & Precedent Adherence Rate & Current decisions aligned with prior analogous decisions. & .25 \\
CDS & Cross-Case Disparity Score & Outcome differences between demographically distinct but legally identical populations; inverted. & .25 \\
DRR & Decision Reversal Rate & Decisions overturned on appeal or review; appellate records; inverted. & .20 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The Case Similarity Engine uses semantic and structured matching while keeping
demographics out of similarity factors, exposes rationale, and requires a
30-case cluster. A protected-class disparity above 15\% creates an equity
cross-flag. Consent decrees remain low-consistency markers until a five-year
clean record; selective retroactive policy application is penalized.

\subsection{Dimension 3: Transparency (\(\Phi_T\))}

\[
T = 0.28\,\mathrm{RDR}+0.25\,\mathrm{RTI}+0.27\,\mathrm{FCR}+0.20\,\mathrm{PAX}
\]

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{1.4cm}p{4cm}p{6.7cm}c}
\toprule
Code & Name & Definition and source basis & Wt. \\
\midrule
RDR & Record Disclosure Rate & Requested records disclosed without contested redactions; FOIA and public-record databases. & .28 \\
RTI & Response Time Index & \(\min(\mathrm{deadline}/\mathrm{median\ actual},1)\). & .25 \\
FCR & Public Records Compliance Rate & Requests fulfilled without appeal or litigation; compelled disclosures are non-compliant. & .27 \\
PAX & Proactive Access Index & Voluntary publication of rationales, changes, and statistics benchmarked to peers. & .20 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The Transparency Crawler verifies availability without login, payment, or
special permission and recrawls every 30 days. A litigation loss sets FCR to
zero for the contested category for 24 months. Receipt, not acknowledgment,
starts the response clock. Exemption-abuse patterns over 25\% appeal overturn
rate reduce RDR by 0.15; contempt for disclosure within five years caps
\(T\) at 0.40.

\subsection{Dimension 4: Efficiency (\(\Phi_E\))}

\[
E = 0.28\,\mathrm{CRR}+0.25\,\mathrm{PTS}+0.22\,\mathrm{RUI}+0.25\,\mathrm{BCR}
\]

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{1.4cm}p{4cm}p{6.7cm}c}
\toprule
Code & Name & Definition and source basis & Wt. \\
\midrule
CRR & Case Resolution Rate & Cases resolved within statutory or regulatory time limit; undocumented administrative closures excluded. & .28 \\
PTS & Processing Time Score & \(\min(\mathrm{benchmark\ median}/\mathrm{agency\ median},1)\). & .25 \\
RUI & Resource Utilization Index & Mandate-advancing outcomes per operating budget, net of litigation costs. & .22 \\
BCR & Backlog Clearance Rate & Year-over-year backlog trajectory: growing below .50, stable .50, reducing above .50. & .25 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Peer benchmarking is mandatory; a peer class below five members produces
\texttt{PEER\_CLASS\_INSUFFICIENT}. A budget cut above 20\% creates a documented
0.10 one-year grace adjustment and degrading performance despite stable or
increasing budgets creates a management-efficiency flag.

\subsection{Dimension 5: Equity (\(\Phi_Q\))}

\[
Q = 0.30(1-\mathrm{DDI})+0.23\,\mathrm{GAS}+0.27\,\mathrm{PCOR}+0.20\,\mathrm{ASR}
\]

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{1.4cm}p{4cm}p{6.7cm}c}
\toprule
Code & Name & Definition and source basis & Wt. \\
\midrule
DDI & Demographic Disparity Index & Outcome-rate differences across protected groups; inverted. & .30 \\
GAS & Geographic Access Score & Physical, digital, and language access across geography. & .23 \\
PCOR & Protected Class Outcome Rate & Favorable outcomes relative to parity, controlling for legally relevant variables. & .27 \\
ASR & Protected Class Appeals Success Rate & Protected-class complainants prevailing on appeal; high rates indicate erroneous adverse decisions. & .20 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The Equity Signal Extractor requires \(n=100\) per demographic cell, never
imputes demographics, and flags statistical significance at \(p<0.05\). Hard
floors override weighted averages: intentional discrimination within ten
years gives \(Q_{\min}=0.15\), an active civil-rights consent decree gives
\(Q_{\min}=0.25\), and a pattern-or-practice finding gives \(Q_{\min}=0.20\).
Language access failure is access failure; Tier V equity triggers referral.

\subsection{Dimension 6: Accountability (\(\Phi_{Ac}\))}

\[
Ac = 0.28\,\mathrm{SCR}+0.25\,\mathrm{AC}+0.22\,\mathrm{CRR_c}+0.25\,\mathrm{RAR}
\]

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{1.4cm}p{4cm}p{6.7cm}c}
\toprule
Code & Name & Definition and source basis & Wt. \\
\midrule
SCR & Self-Correction Rate & Self-initiated correction before external compulsion; proactive multiplier 1.5. & .28 \\
AC & Audit Compliance & Audit recommendations implemented on schedule. & .25 \\
CRR$_c$ & Complaint Resolution Rate & Complaints resolved with documented resolution; distinct from Efficiency CRR. & .22 \\
RAR & Remediation Action Rate & Required remediation completed on schedule and verified. & .25 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The Self-Audit Intelligence Agent detects correction quality, while the
Accountability Enforcement Agent applies a 1.5 multiplier only to proactive
correction and caps \(Ac\) at 0.35 after three consecutive periods of the same
violation. Resolution claims without documentation trigger
\texttt{RESOLUTION\_FRAUD} and a 24-month audit.
