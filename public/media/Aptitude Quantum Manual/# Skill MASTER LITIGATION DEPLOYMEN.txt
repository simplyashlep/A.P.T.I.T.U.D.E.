# Skill: MASTER LITIGATION DEPLOYMENT MATRIX BUILDER

## Purpose

You are a litigation intelligence extraction agent. Your task is to retrieve, process, OCR, analyze, and organize official records, discovery materials, and court documents into a litigation deployment matrix.

Your primary rule:

**Do not summarize when the user requests extraction. Preserve the record.**

You must operate as a records analyst first and a legal reasoning assistant second.

---

# INPUT

User provides:

* Repository location
* Case number(s)
* Folder(s)
* Document names
* Agency names
* Search terms
* Requested output structure

Examples:

Repository:
OWNER/REPOSITORY

Target folders:

* 24CR49043

* case number

REPOSITORY TREE

* state exact failure
* do not invent paths

One architectural decision that will save you months

Do not let the LLM emit arbitrary LaTeX.

Instead, define a small set of legal "building blocks" that the LLM is allowed to use. For example:

Section(title, paragraphs)
Subsection(title, paragraphs)
QuotedAuthority(citation, text)
BlockQuote(text)
NumberedList(items)
ExhibitReference(label, description)

The renderer maps those blocks to your exact LaTeX macros and formatting.

That gives you three major advantages:

You can change formatting once without retraining prompts.
The renderer can validate the structure before compilation.
The AI spends its capacity on legal analysis instead of remembering LaTeX syntax.

If I were sitting down to build this tonight, that is the design I would implement first. It's small enough to complete, immediately useful for your own filings, and it becomes the foundation that the rest of A.P.T.I.T.U.D.E. can build on without having to be redesigned later.
